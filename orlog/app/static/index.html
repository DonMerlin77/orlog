What is Wyrd?
Most people make the same decision five times under different names. Wyrd stops that. Every time you use Loki or run Orlog, it quietly tracks what you keep returning to — the questions you circled but never closed, the tensions that keep showing up, the domains where your heat runs highest. Over sessions, it builds a mirror: not what you decided, but how you decide.

Team Wyrd
When two people run Orlog on related decisions — a co-founder, a partner, a colleague — their patterns can overlap in ways neither sees alone. Team Wyrd surfaces the shared tensions: what you're both circling, what one person resolved that the other hasn't, where you're pulling in opposite directions without knowing it.
How to use it: Share your team code with anyone you make decisions with. Their sessions (not their words — just the pattern signals) merge into a shared view. Useful for co-founders before a big call, couples on a major decision, or any two people who keep having the same argument.
