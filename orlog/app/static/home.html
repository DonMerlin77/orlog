What the output looks like

Question submitted

"I've been offered a promotion at my current company, but I've been quietly building a side project for two years. Do I take the promotion or go all in?"

ᚨ  Anchor

The promotion feels safe — but the real risk is the two years already invested. Staying becomes the gamble, not leaving.

ᛏ  Tension

Financial security vs. ownership of outcome. The promotion buys comfort. The project buys a shot at something that's actually yours.

ᚷ  Gap

Whether the project has any real external validation. One paying customer changes the math entirely. Without it, this is a belief question, not a business one.

ᚱ  Risk

You take the promotion, coast on the salary, and the project dies quietly. In three years you're still here, wondering what would have happened.

ᛈ  Verdict

If the project has zero external signal, take the promotion — then set a 90-day deadline to get one paying customer. If it already has signal, you already know the answer. You're just looking for permission to leave.
